Helpful debugging information

When reporting a problem, please include, when relevant:

  • Your iOS version
  • Your Pausetide version
  • A clear description of what happened and what you expected
  • A screenshot that helps explain the problem

Protect your privacy

Please do not send unnecessary sensitive personal information. Before attaching a screenshot, check that it does not reveal private notifications, account details, contact information, or other information that is not needed to understand the issue.
