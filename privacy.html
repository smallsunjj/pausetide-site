Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Pausetide is designed to help you make more intentional choices about app use while keeping your personal information on your device.

No account or developer data collection

No account is required to use Pausetide. The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information. Pausetide contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking, and it does not use a developer-operated backend.

Screen Time information

Information used through Apple’s Screen Time, Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks is processed locally on your device using Apple-provided frameworks. Screen Time usage and statistics data is not transmitted to the developer.

Personalization images

Images you select for personalization remain local to your device. They are not uploaded or transmitted to the developer.

Premium purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and account information according to Apple’s own policies.

Apple system services

Pausetide relies on Apple system frameworks and App Store services. Apple system services may process information under Apple’s own terms and privacy policies.
